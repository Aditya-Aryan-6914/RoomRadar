How it works

1

Register your college

Create an account for your institute. Your campus gets its own private dashboard link. Nothing is pre-loaded: every room comes from your data.

2

Upload a timetable PDF

Drop in a department timetable. Department name, each division, its classroom, subjects and teachers are read automatically. One PDF can hold many divisions.

3

Dashboard builds itself

Live room cards plus a day timeline per department: red when a class is running with the teacher's name, green when it is free.

What you get

Live status

Free rooms show "free until" so students know how long they can stay. Refreshes every 30 seconds.

Hold a session

Students and faculty can reserve a free room for a group discussion, project work or an extra lecture. Clashes are blocked automatically.

Changes & emergencies

Cancel a class, add a rescheduled one or block a room instantly. Remove the change and the timetable takes over again.

Multi-division PDFs

AIML-A, AIML-B, AIML-C in one file? Each division gets its own classroom and timetable.

Always your data

Replace or delete a timetable at any time. Rooms that no longer have data disappear from the dashboard.

Open JSON API

Every campus exposes /c/<campus>/api/status/ for signage screens or apps.

PDF format that is understood

A grid timetable: time slots across the top (9:00-10:00), weekdays down the left, and each cell as Subject / Teacher / Room (optional). A title line such as Artificial Intelligence - Time Table gives the department and Class: SE-A   Room No: 101 (or Division: B) gives the division and its room. Scanned image-only PDFs are not supported.
